My scooter
Mi Scooter 1S
Used to make the range estimate closer to real life.
3 easy ways to check which model you have:
1. The Mi Home app
Open Mi Home and tap your scooter. The exact name is at the top, e.g. "Mi Electric Scooter 1S".
2. The sticker
Look on the stem (the pole) or under the footboard for a label. It shows the model name and a number like DDHBC02NEB (1S) or DDHBC08NEB (Scooter 4).
3. By looking at it
1S: smaller, 8.5" tyres, no suspension, single rear reflector. The common older model.
Scooter 4: 10" tubeless tyres, turn-signal look, bigger deck.
4 Pro: largest of the "4" family, longest range.
4 Lite: budget version, shorter range.
4 Ultra: biggest, has suspension front and back.
Still unsure? Tell me the number from the sticker and I'll identify it.
